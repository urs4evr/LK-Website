Frequently asked questions

How do I work out which anniversary it is?

Subtract the year you got together or married from the current year. If that date has already passed this year, that number is the anniversary you last celebrated. If it is still to come, it is the one coming up.

What if our date is February 29?

In years without a February 29, this calculator uses February 28. Many couples celebrate on February 28 or March 1, so choose whichever feels right.

How do I find out how long we have been together?

Use the relationship counter. It shows your time together in days, weeks, months and years.
