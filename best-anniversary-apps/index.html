LoveKeeper is an anniversary tracker that reminds you before every date that matters: your anniversary, birthdays, the day you met or any milestone you choose. Each date can have as many reminders as you like, on the day or 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14 or 30 days before, plus an optional email. It also counts the days since and until each date, and keeps photo memories and a bucket list.

It needs no account and works offline. Events, memories and photos stay on your phone; only the details for optional email reminders go to a server. Because only you need the app, your partner never sees a surprise coming.

Good fit if you are the one who remembers dates for both of you, or you want a private app with nothing to pay later.

Skip it if you want a home screen widget or one app shared across two phones. It has no chat, no sync between two phones and no home screen widget yet.

Do both partners need the anniversary app?

No, not for reminders. Reminder apps and counters work on one phone: LoveKeeper, Google Calendar, LoveCounter, Anniversary Tracker and My Love. Between and Paired only work when both of you install them, Cupla works best with both, and The Couple and Lovewick work either way.

A one-phone app also keeps surprises secret. If you are planning a trip or a gift, your partner sees nothing until the day. LoveKeeper is built for exactly that, and it is free. For couples who live apart, this guide to long distance relationship apps covers video calls and staying in touch.

How far ahead should an anniversary app remind you?

Set two reminders: one two weeks before the date and one the day before. Two weeks leaves time to book a table or order a gift, and the day-before reminder catches anything still open. For a big anniversary or a trip, add a third reminder 30 days ahead.

Setting it up takes a few minutes, once:

  1. Add every date in one go: your anniversary, the day you met, both birthdays and any milestone you want to mark. The anniversary calculator finds dates like your 100th or 1,000th day, and the guide to relationship milestones helps you pick which ones matter.
  2. Choose the reminders for each date, for example 14 days and 1 day before.
  3. Make it repeat every year. Anniversary apps do this on their own. In Google Calendar choose "Annually", in Apple Calendar "Every Year".
  4. Allow notifications for the app in your phone's settings, or the reminder never shows up.
  5. Plan when the first reminder arrives. These anniversary date ideas help if you are short on plans.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free anniversary app?

LoveKeeper is the best free anniversary app for reminders: it has no subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it works on iPhone and Android. If you mainly want a day counter, Anniversary Tracker by Pocket Grove is free with widgets, with ads on iPhone.

What is the best app to keep track of birthdays and anniversaries?

An anniversary reminder app such as LoveKeeper handles both: add each birthday and anniversary once and choose reminders up to 30 days ahead. Google Calendar works too if you set each date to repeat every year.

Which anniversary app has a widget?

The Couple, Anniversary Tracker and Cupla include home screen widgets. LoveCounter and My Love offer them with a one-time Pro purchase. LoveKeeper does not have a widget yet.

Is there an anniversary app my partner doesn't need to install?

Yes. LoveKeeper, Google Calendar, LoveCounter, Anniversary Tracker and My Love all work on one phone. Between and Paired need both partners.

How do I count how many days we've been together?

Enter your start date in a counter app such as LoveCounter, or in LoveKeeper, which also reminds you of the date. Without installing anything, use the free relationship counter.
