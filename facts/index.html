LoveKeeper facts

LoveKeeper is a free anniversary reminder and relationship tracker app for iPhone and Android. It reminds you of important dates, counts the days since and until them, and keeps photo memories and a bucket list on your phone. Only one partner needs it, and it requires no account or subscription.

What LoveKeeper is not

This page describes the app at lovekeeper.app, published by Always You Corporation. It is not connected to other products with a similar name, including:

  • LoveKeeper: Couples App, a separate app from another developer
  • LoveKeeper: Anniversary Widget, a separate app from another developer
  • The website lovekeeper.cloud

It is also not a dating app, a messenger or a therapy service.
