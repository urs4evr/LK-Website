6 Month Anniversary: When It Is and How to Mark It

Your 6 month anniversary falls on the same day number, six months after the day you got together. If you started on May 12, it is November 12. If you started on the 29th, 30th or 31st and the target month is shorter, it moves to that month's last day. Six months are 181 to 184 days, about 26 weeks.

When is your 6-month anniversary?

Your 6-month anniversary is the date six calendar months after your start date, with the same day number. Only starts late in the month need a rule, because some months end on the 30th and February ends on the 28th or 29th. Then the last day of the shorter month counts.

You got together onYour 6-month anniversary
The 1st to the 28th of any monthSame day number, six months later (May 12 → November 12)
August 29February 28 (February 29 in a leap year)
August 30 or 31February 28 (February 29 in a leap year)
March 31September 30
May 31November 30
October 31April 30
December 31June 30

All other starts on the 29th to 31st keep their day number. A start on January 31 lands on July 31, for example. If you started on August 30 or 31 and prefer March 1, that works too, as long as you both mean the same day.

The same rule works for your one-month and three-month anniversaries. One month after January 31 is February 28 (or 29). Three months after November 30 is February 28 too. For the yearly date and the weekday it falls on, use the anniversary calculator.

How many days is 6 months?

Six months are 181 to 184 days, depending on the months in between. February and the 31-day months make the difference.

  • 181 days: for example from January 1 to July 1, or from November 22 to May 22, because February is inside.
  • 182 or 183 days: for example from January 1 to July 1 in a leap year (182), or from April 1 to October 1 (183).
  • 184 days: for example from March 1 to September 1, when July and August sit back to back with 31 days each.

So "about 182 days" is a good rule of thumb, but not your exact count. If you want to celebrate the day count instead, the relationship counter shows how many days you have been together.

Is a 6-month anniversary a big deal?

Six months is a real milestone, but a small one. By now you know a lot of each other's everyday habits. Many couples also hit a few "firsts" around this time. Whether it deserves a celebration is up to the two of you.

A YouGov survey of 1,323 US adults (July 2021) asked people who were married or in a serious relationship when they reached certain steps. Each figure below refers only to those who had already reached that step:

StepAfter 1 to 3 monthsAfter 4 to 6 months
Said "I love you"26 %17 %
Introduced a partner to family, or met theirs25 %18 %
First trip together12 %17 %

So by six months, many couples have said "I love you" and met family. The first trip together came more often in months four to six than in the first three months. If some of these steps are still ahead of you, that is normal too. The same survey shows a wide range of timelines.

You may also hear that six months is when the "honeymoon phase" fades or a slump begins. That is a rule of thumb from dating advice, not a measured fact. If you notice more friction now, it often helps to talk about it openly. A milestone date is a natural moment for that talk.

How big should you go at six months?

Go smaller than you plan for one year, and agree on the size first. A quick question a week ahead works well: "Our six months is next Friday. Want to do something?" Then you avoid the classic mismatch where one of you brings a gift and the other forgot the date.

What fits six months:

  • A date with a link to your story: the place of your first date, the dish you cooked together, the walk you took on day one.
  • Something you make together: a playlist of your first six months, a short photo album, a letter for each other.
  • A look ahead: pick three things you want to do in the next six months and put the first one in the calendar.

Married for six months? The same date rule applies to your wedding date. A dinner out, a look at your wedding photos or a letter to each other fits the size of the day.

What do you say on your 6-month anniversary?

Say one specific thing from the six months, then one thing you look forward to. That beats a long quote, because only the two of you can write it. Some lines to adapt:

  • "Six months ago I had no idea you'd become my favorite part of the week. Happy six months."
  • "Half a year of you, and I still look forward to every Friday. Here's to the next six months."
  • "Six months, one terrible first movie choice and a lot of good mornings. Happy half-anniversary."
  • "Thank you for the last six months. I'd like to plan the next trip with you."
  • For a text in the morning: "Happy 6 months. Tonight is on me."

If you are not sure your partner marks the date, say it lightly. A warm text never feels like too much, even if you didn't plan anything else.

How do you make sure you don't miss it?

Set a reminder about a week before. That gives you time to book a table or write a card, and a second reminder the day before keeps it in mind.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 6-month anniversary called?

Most people simply say 6-month anniversary. Some call it a half-anniversary. In the Philippines, couples often call it their sixth monthsary.

Is it weird to celebrate a 6-month anniversary?

No. Plenty of couples mark six months, and plenty skip it. Ask your partner whether they want to mark the day. Then keep it smaller than what you plan for one year.

How many days is 6 months?

Six calendar months are 181 to 184 days, or about 26 weeks. The exact number depends on which months fall in between. Starting on January 1 gives 181 days, and starting on March 1 gives 184.

When is my 6-month anniversary if we started on August 31?

It falls on February 28, or February 29 in a leap year. February has no 30th or 31st, so the last day of the month stands in. The same is true for starts on August 29 and 30.

Do you give a gift for a 6-month anniversary?

You don't have to. A card, a shared plan or a small, personal gift fits six months well. Save the bigger gift for your first anniversary, so the one-year mark still feels special.

How do you say happy 6-month anniversary?

Name something real from the six months. For example: Six months ago I had no idea you'd become my favorite part of the week. Happy six months. A specific memory says more than a long quote.
