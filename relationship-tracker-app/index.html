Relationship Tracker App: Dates, Memories and Goals

A relationship tracker app keeps the dates, moments and plans of your relationship in one place on your phone. Most apps only count the days since you got together and remind you of your anniversary. A fuller tracker also holds birthdays of the people you love, photo memories with notes and a bucket list of things you want to do together.

What can a relationship tracker app track besides days?

A relationship tracker app can track four things, and most apps cover only the first two:

  1. Time together: days, weeks, months and years since a start date, often down to the second.
  2. Dates ahead: anniversaries, birthdays and other yearly days, with a reminder before each one.
  3. Memories: photos, a title and a few lines about a trip, a celebration or a first.
  4. Plans: a bucket list of places, experiences and goals you want to reach together, ticked off when done.

The first two answer "how long?" and "what is next?". The last two answer "what have we done?" and "what do we still want to do?". That is the part a day counter leaves out, and it is the part you can scroll back through years later.

Can a relationship tracker app remind you of family and friends' birthdays?

Yes, if it lets you add more than one person's dates. Many love counters stop at your anniversary and maybe your partner's birthday. In LoveKeeper, every date gets a category: Love, Birthday, Memorial or Other. A birthday can be tagged as your partner's, a child's or a friend's, and you can add parents, siblings and anyone else as a birthday without a tag.

Dates worth adding beyond your anniversary:

  • Your partner: birthday, the day you met, your first date, the day you moved in or got engaged, if those apply.
  • Their people: birthdays of your partner's parents, siblings and close friends, so you no longer depend on your partner to mention them.
  • Your people: family and friends whose birthdays you want to catch in time for a card or a call.
  • Memorial days: the anniversary of a loss, so the day does not catch either of you off guard.
  • Yearly days you celebrate: Valentine's Day, a holiday you both keep or a yearly trip.

Each date can have as many reminders as you like, on the day or 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14 or 30 days before. A birthday that needs a gift sent by mail gets 14 days; a call to a friend gets 1. Reminders for love and birthday dates can include gift ideas for the occasion.

How do you keep relationship memories in an app?

Keep a memory as a short entry with a date, a title, a few lines and photos. In LoveKeeper, each memory holds up to 10 photos and a category: Travel, Celebration, Milestone or Other. You can sort the list by newest or oldest first, which turns it into a timeline of your relationship.

A few habits keep the album useful:

  • Write the detail you will forget. The photo shows the beach; the note says why you laughed all evening.
  • Add the memory the same week. Small moments fade faster than big trips.
  • Use Milestone for firsts: first trip, first apartment, first holiday with each other's family.

Events, memories and photos stay on your phone. LoveKeeper has no cloud sync, so make a backup file from time to time, especially before you change phones.

How does a couples bucket list work in a tracker app?

A couples bucket list is a running list of things you want to do together, and the app keeps it next to your dates and memories. In LoveKeeper, each item has a title, an optional description and photo, a category (Travel, Experience, Goal or Other) and a priority from High to Low. When you have done it, mark it complete and the date is saved.

Good items are specific enough to plan:

  • Travel: "A weekend in Savannah" instead of "travel more".
  • Experience: a cooking class, a sunrise hike, a night with no phones.
  • Goal: save for a trip, learn a dance together, run a 5K side by side.

If you are stuck, the Show Ideas button suggests items to start with. Mark the next one or two as High, so the list turns into plans and not just wishes.

How do you set up a relationship tracker app in 10 minutes?

Start with the dates, then add one memory and three bucket list items. In LoveKeeper:

  1. Add your start date as a Love date. The app shows the days since and the days until the next anniversary.
  2. Add birthdays: your partner's first, then family and friends, tagged where it helps.
  3. Set reminders for each date, for example 7 days and 1 day before.
  4. Add one memory with a photo from your phone, such as your first trip.
  5. Add three bucket list items and give one of them High priority.

You need no account, and email is only needed if you want reminders by email as well. Only one of you needs the app, so you can set it up as a surprise. LoveKeeper is free on iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later) and Android, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best relationship tracker app?

It depends on what you want to keep. If you only want a live day count on your home screen, a counter with a widget such as Been Love Memory or Relationship Tracker+ fits. If you also want birthdays of family and friends, photo memories and a shared bucket list in one free app without ads, LoveKeeper covers those, but it has no widget yet.

Is there a free relationship tracker app?

Yes. Love Counter, Been Love Memory, Relationship Tracker+ and LoveKeeper are all free to download. Been Love Memory shows ads and sells in-app purchases. LoveKeeper has no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do both partners need the app?

Not with LoveKeeper. One person adds the dates, memories and bucket list, and nothing syncs to a second phone. If you want a shared space that both of you edit, look for a couples app with partner sync instead.

What apps can I use to track my spouse?

Apps that show where your partner is are location-sharing tools, such as Find My on iPhone or location sharing in Google Maps. Use them only when both of you agree and either of you can turn sharing off. A relationship tracker app tracks your dates and memories, not a person.

What is the 7 7 7 rule for couples?

The 7-7-7 rule is a popular rule of thumb: a date every 7 days, a night or weekend away every 7 weeks and a vacation every 7 months. It is not based on research, and many couples adapt the intervals to their budget and schedule. A bucket list is a good place to collect ideas for each step.
