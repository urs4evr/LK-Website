What does an anniversary reminder app do?

An anniversary reminder app keeps your important dates in one list and warns you ahead of each one. Most also show how many days are left and how long ago a date was. That is the difference to a phone calendar, which sends a nudge between work meetings and nothing else.

You wantPhone calendarAnniversary reminder app
A notification before the dateYes, one or two alertsYes, several per date
Time to plan (weeks, not hours)Limited alert choicesUp to 30 days ahead
Days left and days sinceNoYes
Photos and memories with the dateNoYes, in LoveKeeper
Dates kept apart from work eventsNoYes

If you only want one nudge a year for one date, your calendar is enough. Once you track several dates, or you want a reminder weeks ahead, a dedicated app is easier to keep up.

How far ahead should an anniversary reminder go off?

Set at least two reminders: one far enough ahead to book or order something, and one close to the day. A reminder on the morning of your anniversary leaves time for a card at best. This plan works for most couples, and you can drop the steps you do not need.

ReminderWhat it gives you time for
30 days beforeBook a restaurant, a weekend away or tickets. Order a personalized or engraved gift
14 days beforeOrder a gift that ships. Arrange a babysitter or pet sitter if you need one
7 days beforeConfirm the booking, plan the day, buy a card
2 days beforeWrap the gift, write the card, check the time and place
1 day beforeTell your partner what to keep free, or keep the surprise and just be ready

Delivery times differ a lot between shops, so check the estimate on the product page before you rely on the 14-day reminder. For gift ideas by year, the guide to romantic anniversary gift ideas lists traditional and modern options.

Which dates should you add to an anniversary reminder app?

Add every date you would feel bad about missing, not only the big anniversary. Each one can have its own reminders.

  • Your relationship anniversary: the day you got together, if you count it.
  • Your wedding anniversary: if you are married, often the most important one.
  • Your partner's birthday: worth a reminder weeks ahead if you like to plan a gift or a party.
  • The day you met or your first date: small dates like these make a surprise more personal.
  • Valentine's Day: LoveKeeper offers it as a quick-add date, so you do not have to type it in.
  • Milestones: your 100th day, 6 months or 1,000 days together. The anniversary calculator shows the exact dates.
  • Family and friends: birthdays of the people your partner cares about, so you can remind each other.

How do you set up anniversary reminders in LoveKeeper?

You add a date once, pick when you want to be reminded, and LoveKeeper repeats the reminders every year. It takes about a minute per date.

  1. Add the date. Tap to add an event, give it a name such as "Our anniversary" and enter the original date.
  2. Choose a category. Love, birthday, memorial or other. This keeps your list easy to scan.
  3. Add reminders. Pick 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14 or 30 days before. Add as many as you like, for example 30, 7 and 1.
  4. Pick the time. Choose the hour each reminder arrives, so it does not wake you or land in the middle of a meeting.
  5. Turn on email, if you want. An optional email reminder a week before works even if you swipe away a notification. You confirm your address first.
  6. Back up your list. Your dates stay on your phone. Save a backup file so you keep them when you change phones.

LoveKeeper also counts the days since and until each date, and keeps photo memories and a bucket list. Reminders for love and birthday dates can include gift ideas for the occasion.

Can you use your phone calendar as an anniversary reminder?

Yes, a calendar works for a single date if you set it up carefully. Create the event as all-day, set it to repeat every year, and add an alert well before the day.

  • iPhone Calendar: create an all-day event, set Repeat to Every Year, then choose an Alert. For all-day events the earliest alert is one week before, and you can add a Second Alert.
  • Google Calendar: create an all-day event, set it to repeat Annually, then add notifications. You can add several and set them in days or weeks ahead.

The limits show up once you track more than one date. All-day alerts stop at a week on iPhone, they mix with work events, and nothing counts the days or keeps your memories. That is when an app like LoveKeeper saves effort.

What should a good anniversary reminder app have?

A good anniversary reminder app warns you early, works for one person and is clear about price. Check these points before you add your dates.

  • Reminders weeks ahead: at least 14 days, ideally 30, and more than one per date.
  • Only you need it: some couple apps only work once both partners sign up and pair accounts.
  • An honest price: many reminder and counter apps are free to download but list in-app purchases or subscriptions in the store.
  • Your data on your phone: and a way to back it up.

What LoveKeeper does not do

LoveKeeper is built for one job: remembering your dates and giving you time to plan. It has no chat, no shared calendar and no sync between two phones. There are no home screen widgets yet, so the day counts live inside the app. If you want a messenger for two, a couple app such as Between fits better, and Between vs. LoveKeeper shows the differences.

Is there a free anniversary reminder app?

Yes. LoveKeeper is free with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads. Your phone calendar is free too, but it only sends a notification and does not count days or keep memories.

How far in advance should I set an anniversary reminder?

Set at least two: one about 30 days before, so you can book a table or order a personalized gift, and one 2 to 7 days before, to confirm plans and write a card. A reminder only on the day is often too late to plan anything.

Does my partner need the app too?

No. LoveKeeper works fully on one phone, so you can plan a surprise without your partner seeing it. If you both want the app, each of you keeps your own list, because the app does not sync between phones.

Can I get anniversary reminders by email?

Yes, in LoveKeeper you can turn on an optional email reminder a week before each date. You confirm your email address first. All other reminders arrive as notifications on your phone and work without an account.

Can an anniversary reminder app remind me of birthdays too?

Yes. LoveKeeper keeps anniversaries, birthdays, first dates and any other yearly date in one list, each with its own reminders. Fixed dates such as Valentine's Day can be added with one tap.
