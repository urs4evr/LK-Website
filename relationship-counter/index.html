How do you count how long you have been together?

Count the calendar days from your start date to today. The start day itself is day 0, so the day after you got together is 1 day. That is the number at the top of the counter, and it is the same number you get when you subtract one date from the other.

For years, months and days, count in this order:

  1. Complete years: how often your date has come around again. March 14, 2023 to March 14, 2026 is 3 years.
  2. Complete months since the last anniversary: March 14 to October 14 is 7 months.
  3. Days left over: October 14 to November 2 is 19 days.

So on November 2, 2026, a couple together since March 14, 2023 has been together 3 years, 7 months and 19 days. That is 1,329 days, 43 months or 189 weeks.

What if you got together on the 29th, 30th or 31st?

Months have different lengths, so late start dates need a rule. This counter uses the last day of a shorter month. If you got together on January 31, your first month is complete on February 28 (February 29 in a leap year), and your second on March 31.

Why doesn't the counter show hours and seconds?

Many love counter apps tick by the second. That only means something if you know the hour you got together. This counter counts whole calendar days, so a time zone or daylight saving time can never shift your number.

Does the first day count as day 1?

That depends on the convention, and it is why two counters can be one day apart. This counter and spreadsheets treat the start day as day 0, the same as subtracting one date from the other. Many couples in South Korea, where the 100th day (baek-il) is a big celebration, count the start day as day 1.

How you countDay 100 if you started January 1, 2026
Start day is day 0 (days since)Saturday, April 11, 2026
Start day is day 1 (Korean style)Friday, April 10, 2026

Neither is wrong. If you count the Korean way, add one day to the total at the top, and your round numbers fall one day earlier than shown. Agree on one way with your partner, and you will celebrate on the same day.

Use the date the two of you think of as the start. There is no official rule, and couples choose differently:

How do you count days together in Google Sheets or Excel?

Put your start date in cell A1 and use these formulas. They work in Google Sheets and Excel and update every day.

  • Days together:
    =DAYS(TODAY(),A1)
  • Complete years:
    =DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"Y")
  • Months after the last full year:
    =DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"YM")
  • Days after the last full month:
    =DAYS(TODAY(),EDATE(A1,DATEDIF(A1,TODAY(),"M")))

Skip DATEDIF with "MD" for the days: Microsoft's own help page warns that it can return wrong results. For start dates on the 29th to 31st, a spreadsheet may complete a month a day or two later than this counter, because it waits for the same day number.

Frequently asked questions

What is a relationship counter?

A relationship counter, also called a love counter, shows how long two people have been together, counted from a start date they choose. This one shows the total days, the years, months and days, the weeks, your next anniversary and your next round number of days.

How many days have we been together?

Enter your start date in the counter at the top of this page, and it shows the number of days right away. To count by hand, subtract your start date from today's date. The start day itself counts as day 0.

How many years is 1,000 days together?

1,000 days is about 2.74 years: 2 years, 8 months and 24 to 28 days, depending on your start date.

What if we got together on February 29?

In years without a February 29, this counter puts your anniversary on February 28. Some couples prefer March 1, so pick the day that feels right to you.
