How do you calculate how long you have been together?

Count the calendar days from your start date to today. For years and months, count complete years first, then complete months, then the remaining days. That is why a couple together since January 31 reaches one month on the last day of February.

Frequently asked questions

Which date should we use as our start date?

Use the date that means the most to the two of you, such as your first date, the day you became official or your first kiss. There is no rule, so you can also pick one and keep the others as separate milestones.

Is this relationship counter free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser. Your date is saved only in your browser so the counter still shows it next time.

How many years is 1,000 days together?

1,000 days is about 2 years and 9 months, or 2.74 years.

Is there an app that counts days together?

Yes. LoveKeeper shows the days since and the days until each of your dates, and reminds you before they arrive. It is free for iPhone and Android. It has no chat, no sync between two phones and no home screen widget yet.
