Questions and Answers

What does the LoveKeeper anniversary tracker do?

It keeps all your important dates in one place, such as anniversaries, birthdays and first dates, and shows how many days have passed and how many are left. You can add as many reminders per date as you like, from 30 days before to the day itself, plus an optional email reminder. Photo memories and a bucket list are included.

Is LoveKeeper really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads.

Does my partner need to download it?

No. LoveKeeper works on one phone, which also makes it useful for planning surprises. If you both want it, each of you keeps your own list, because phones do not sync yet.

Do I need an account?

No. You only give an email address if you want email reminders, and you confirm it first.

Where is my data stored?

Events, memories and photos stay on your phone, and the core features work offline. If you turn on email reminders, the event details needed to send them are stored on a server. You can back up everything to a file.

How is it different from a phone calendar?

A calendar sends a nudge. LoveKeeper also counts the days since and until each date, keeps your memories with photos, and lets you set several early reminders so there is time to plan something.

Does LoveKeeper have a home screen widget?

Not yet. Today the day counters are inside the app.
