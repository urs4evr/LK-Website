Which "My Love" app do you have?

The famous one is My Love – Relationship Counter by Bjoern Bartels, App Store id 409609608, on the store since 2010 and rated more than a million times. This page compares LoveKeeper with that app.

At least five other apps use "My Love" in their name, from different developers. Some are on Android, some have subscriptions or mood trackers. If your app runs on Android, it is not the Bartels app, because that one is iPhone only. Even some review sites mix them up, so check the developer name in your store before you compare features.

What My Love does well

My Love shows your time together in years, months and days, with a couple photo as the background and notifications for special day counts. Its familiar layout makes the relationship start date the center of the app.

LoveKeeper builds on that everyday reason to check your dates: keep the day count, add the other occasions you care about, and choose reminders before each one.

Where My Love falls short

  • One relationship date. The app is built around the day you got together. There is no list for a wedding anniversary, a partner's birthday or the day you moved in. Wanting more countdowns is a common wish in its reviews.
  • No updates since May 2023. The last version, 2.3.2, fixed a typo in the widget. Before that, the app saw a few updates a year at most.
  • iPhone only. There is no Android version, so a partner on Android cannot use the same app.
  • The widget costs extra. The home screen widget is part of the My Love Pro in-app purchase, $0.99 in the US store.
  • Rating prompts. Some users say the app asks for a rating too often when they only want a quick look.

What LoveKeeper does differently

LoveKeeper is an anniversary tracker app for every date in your relationship, not only the first one. You add your anniversary, first date, wedding day, birthdays or any trip you are looking forward to. Each date shows the days since or the days left.

The bigger difference is reminders. For each date you choose as many reminders as you like, on the day or 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14 or 30 days before, so there is time to plan a gift or book a table. Reminders for love dates and birthdays can include gift ideas for the occasion.

You can also keep photo memories and a bucket list of things you want to do together. Everything works offline, stays on your phone and can be saved as a backup file. Only one of you needs the app, which keeps surprises secret.

What LoveKeeper does not have yet: a home screen widget (it is the next feature), sync between two phones, and chat. It is in English only.

How to switch from My Love to LoveKeeper

  1. Open My Love and note your start date, plus any special days you care about.
  2. Install LoveKeeper and add the start date as your anniversary. The days-since count appears right away.
  3. Add the other dates, such as birthdays or your wedding day, and choose reminders for each.
  4. Keep My Love until you have seen your first LoveKeeper reminder arrive, then decide whether you still need it.

If the two apps show a count one day apart, they start counting differently. The guide to counting days together explains day 0 versus day 1.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to My Love?

Yes. LoveKeeper is free with no in-app purchases. It tracks as many dates as you like, reminds you before each one and keeps photo memories and a bucket list. It has no home screen widget yet.

Is My Love – Relationship Counter on Android?

No. The My Love app by Bjoern Bartels is iPhone only. Android apps with "My Love" in their name come from other developers. LoveKeeper runs on both iPhone and Android.

Is My Love still updated?

Its last App Store update, version 2.3.2, came out in May 2023 and fixed a typo in the widget.

What is My Love Pro?

My Love Pro is the app's $0.99 in-app purchase in the US App Store. It adds the home screen widget.

Can I track more than one date in My Love?

My Love is built around one relationship start date. To track birthdays, a wedding day or other anniversaries as well, use an app with a list of dates, such as LoveKeeper.

Does my partner need the app too?

No, neither app needs your partner to install it. In LoveKeeper only one person needs the app, and there is no sync between two phones.
