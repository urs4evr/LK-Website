What is the Between app?

Between is a private messenger for couples by DLT Partners, on the App Store since 2011. You and your partner connect your accounts, then chat, call, share photos and videos, and plan in a shared calendar. The calendar counts down to your special days, and the app shows how long you have been together.

The app is free with ads. Between Plus removes the ads and adds themes, love letters and longer video and voice messages, for both of you. In the US App Store it costs $2.99 a month or $26.99 once; the yearly price shows as $13.99 in the description and $24.99 in the purchase list. Stickers are bought separately with in-app "Hearts".

What Between does well

Between gives conversations a couple theme, with stickers, themes and a shared album. It has been around since 2011 and combines messaging with a calendar for two connected accounts.

LoveKeeper takes a simpler approach to remembering dates: you can use it on your own phone, keep photo memories and plan a surprise without moving your conversations to a new service.

Where Between falls short as an anniversary tracker

  • Both of you have to join. Between only works once your partner installs it and moves your conversations there. If your partner is happy with the chat app you already use, that is a hard sell.
  • Ads unless you pay. The free version shows ads. Removing them takes a subscription or the $26.99 lifetime purchase.
  • Reminders are not the focus. Special days sit in a shared calendar. There is no list of dates with several reminders, say a week and a day before, so you have time to plan a gift or book a table.
  • No surprises. Everything in the shared space is visible to your partner, which makes it hard to plan something secret.
  • Your data lives in the service. Between's App Store privacy label lists data used to track you and data linked to you for third-party advertising. Its listings say your data is encrypted.
  • Extras cost extra. Some users dislike that stickers come from a paid "Hearts" store.

What LoveKeeper does differently

LoveKeeper is an anniversary tracker app that one person uses alone. You add your anniversary, first date, wedding day, birthdays or a trip you are looking forward to. Each date shows the days since or the days left.

For each date you choose as many reminders as you like, on the day or 1, 2, 3, 7, 10, 14 or 30 days before. Reminders for love dates and birthdays can include gift ideas for the occasion. Because your partner does not see the app, a surprise stays a surprise.

You can also keep photo memories and a bucket list. LoveKeeper is free with no ads, no subscription and no account. Events, memories and photos stay on your phone, it works offline, and you can save a backup file.

What LoveKeeper does not have: chat, calls, a shared calendar, sync between two phones and a home screen widget. It is in English only.

How to move your dates from Between to LoveKeeper

  1. Open the special days in Between's calendar and note the dates you care about, starting with the day you got together.
  2. Install LoveKeeper and add that day as your anniversary. The days-since count appears right away.
  3. Add birthdays, your wedding day and other dates, and choose reminders for each.
  4. Keep Between for chatting if you like it. If you leave it, save the photos you want to keep first, because LoveKeeper cannot import them.

If the two apps show a count one day apart, they start counting differently. The anniversary calculator shows the exact dates for 100 days, 1,000 days and every year.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Between alternative that my partner does not need?

Yes. LoveKeeper works on one phone. It reminds you before your anniversary and other dates and keeps photo memories, but it has no chat.

Is Between free?

Between is free with ads. Between Plus removes the ads and adds extras for both partners, from $2.99 a month or $26.99 once in the US App Store.

Does Between work if only one partner has it?

No. Between connects two accounts, so both partners need the app.

Can I use Between and LoveKeeper together?

Yes. Keep chatting in Between and use LoveKeeper on your own phone for reminders. The apps do not depend on each other.

Can I import my Between data into LoveKeeper?

No. Add your dates in LoveKeeper by hand, which takes a few minutes, and save any photos from Between before you leave it.

Is LoveKeeper really free?

Yes. LoveKeeper has no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads and no account.
