How many days until Valentine's Day?

The number at the top is the answer for today. It counts whole calendar days, so tomorrow it is one lower, and on February 14 it says "It's today!". The day after Valentine's Day, the countdown starts again for the next year.

The weeks line answers the other common question, how many weeks until Valentine's Day. Six weeks or more is plenty of time. Under two weeks, anything that needs shipping or a reservation gets tight.

What day of the week is Valentine's Day?

February 14 moves one weekday each year, and two after a leap day. Here are the next five years:

YearFebruary 14 falls onWhat it means for plans
2027SundayA whole weekend: Saturday evening and Sunday both work
2028MondayMany couples celebrate on Saturday, February 12
2029WednesdayA weeknight: plan something short, or move it to the weekend
2030ThursdayWeeknight again; Friday the 15th is an easy alternative
2031FridayPopular evening, book early

A weekend Valentine's Day usually means more people go out on the same night. If you want a restaurant or a trip on February 13 or 14, 2027, book sooner than you would for a Wednesday.

How can you count down to Valentine's Day together?

A countdown can be part of the gift. Pick a start, such as 14 days or the week of February 7, and do one small thing each day. A few that cost little or nothing:

  • A note a day: one sentence about something you like about your partner, left where they will find it.
  • Photo countdown: send one photo from your time together each day, oldest first.
  • Song a day: build a shared playlist, one song per day, and play it on February 14.
  • Question a day: ask one question you have never asked before and talk about it over dinner.
  • Envelopes: numbered envelopes with small surprises or date coupons, one opened per day.

Long distance? Send the notes and photos by message, and schedule a video call for the evening of the 14th. If you want to know how long you have been together by then, the relationship counter gives you the exact number of days for a note.

Do you have to celebrate Valentine's Day?

No. Some couples skip it, celebrate a day later when it is quieter, or keep their own dates instead, such as the day they met. Talk about it before the countdown gets short, so neither of you guesses. Friends often celebrate Galentine's Day on February 13, a custom that comes from the TV show Parks and Recreation.

If your own dates matter more to you, the anniversary calculator shows your next anniversary and round days, and relationship milestones has ideas for marking them.

Why is Valentine's Day on February 14?

February 14 is the feast day of Saint Valentine in the Christian calendar. The link to romantic love is usually traced to medieval England, where Geoffrey Chaucer wrote in the 14th century about birds choosing their mates on "Seynt Valentynes day". Exchanging cards and gifts became common much later.

Frequently asked questions

What day is Valentine's Day in 2027?

Valentine's Day 2027 is on Sunday, February 14.

Is Valentine's Day always on February 14?

Yes. The date never changes, but the weekday moves each year.

How many weeks until Valentine's Day?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the weeks and days left, updated every day.

When should I book a Valentine's Day dinner?

About four weeks ahead is a safe rule, earlier when February 14 falls on a weekend, as it does in 2027.

Is Galentine's Day on February 13?

Yes. Galentine's Day, a day to celebrate friends, is on February 13, the day before Valentine's Day.
